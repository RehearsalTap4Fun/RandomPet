## Method

All 20 fixed inputs (`2026082101` through `2026082120`) were generated through public `generateMonster` calls without a rig override or injected rig. Themes cycle `deep-sea`, `fungal`, `shadow`. Each spec was rendered through the product `PreviewCanvas` and Canvas renderer in bundled Chromium 151.0.7922.34 at 1024×1024 with transparency. Sharp was used only to assemble the numbered contact sheet.

The contact sheet was inspected as a whole, and all 20 source PNGs were inspected at full resolution. The nine required criteria are abbreviated below:

- Limb: limb and appendage continuity.
- Ground: credible ground contact or intentional floating suspension.
- Pen.: no accidental penetration.
- Layer: correct foreground/background ordering.
- Camera/light: centered crop, readable silhouette, coherent light.
- Face/persp.: face and appendages share the intended perspective.
- Material/palette: coherent material response and palette application.
- Weird: friendly-weird appeal remains readable.
- Theme: requested theme remains visually readable.

`PASS` is a technical assessment. It does not replace the pending human approval.

## Defect found and corrected during inspection

The first generated set exposed narrow barcode-like palette streaks at several deep-sea body edges. Investigation traced them to isolated shaded/highlight clusters in color-mask k-means output being treated as full color-role seeds and propagated across transparent gaps. A regression test reproduced the fragmented connected components. The mask builder now retains the largest connected component for each approved role before filling unlabeled pixels; all 27 production palette masks and their catalog evidence hashes were regenerated. The complete 20-entry set was then regenerated and re-inspected. No streaking remains in the recorded candidate.
